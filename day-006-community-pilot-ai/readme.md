# Community Pilot AI

### AI-powered coordination for community food rescue

Community Pilot AI helps communities move surplus food to people who need it by connecting **food donors, community organizations, and volunteers** in one operational workflow.

Built for the **AI for Social Good Hackathon**, Community Pilot goes beyond a dashboard or chatbot. It combines operational data, AI-powered matching, community intelligence, and voice outreach to help coordinators determine **what should happen next**.

# The Problem

Food rescue is often a coordination problem.

A restaurant may have dozens of meals available for only a few hours. At the same time, a community organization may need those meals, while volunteers are available to transport them.

The information exists — but coordinating:

- Who has the food?
- Who needs it?
- Who can transport it?
- Who is available right now?
- Who can carry enough?
- Who is close enough?
- What food expires first?
- Who should be contacted?
- What happens if the volunteer doesn't answer?

can quickly become a manual and time-sensitive process.

**Community Pilot AI turns those disconnected pieces into one operational workflow.**
